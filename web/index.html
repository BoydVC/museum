How to walk the museum

Desktop: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk (← → turn). Drag with the mouse to look around. Click the floor to walk there. Click a painting to see it full-screen. T starts or stops the auto-tour.

Phone: tap the floor to walk there, swipe left or right to turn, swipe up or down to walk. Tap a painting to open it.
